Check what Recipes works out

Recipes reads, works out and suggests things for you: a recipe read from a photo, ingredients read from a fridge photo, amounts in other units, weights, swaps, diet and spice labels, pantry matches, cooking temperatures and meal plans. These are made to help, but they can be wrong.

  • Check amounts, temperatures and times against the original before you cook, especially from a scan.
  • Allergies and diets: diet labels and swaps are worked out from the ingredient names and are not medical or nutritional advice. If you or someone you cook for has an allergy or a medical diet, check every ingredient and label yourself.
  • Food safety is up to the cook: cook meat to a safe temperature and store food safely, whatever a recipe says.
